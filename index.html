How it plays

Hide, guess, and race to Ra

No dice. The whole game turns on one bluff: how many marbles are in your fist?

I

Hide the fist

Secretly close 1–5 marbles in your hand. Your opponent guesses the number. Guess wrong and you move; guess right and the turn passes to them.

II

Unleash a lion

Instead of moving on a winning guess, set a lion on a rival pharaoh to freeze it in place until it can bluff its way loose.

III

Reach the sanctuary

Lead every pharaoh from the serpent's tail to its head at the center — the sanctuary of Ra. First to bring them all home wins.
